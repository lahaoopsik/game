# Agreed direction and next resources

- Bright original pixel fantasy, restrained foliage saturation.
- Light affects tree colors; daytime and nighttime, animated nature and props.
- Authored map with fixed elevations, like the terrain structure in Stardew Valley.
  Player builds on existing terrain and cannot manually change height.
- Pixel fantasy interface: health, bond capacity, clock, active companion;
  nine item slots below; Tab inventory.
- Initial inventory has nine shared slots, not nine extra hotbar slots.
  Future backpacks expand capacity; bonuses and crafting are not decided.
- Health, bond capacity and companion display are placeholders until those
  gameplay systems exist. Current real inventory resource is wood.

## Next resource work

User explicitly wants stone and iron deposits distributed on the map.
Add their collection, inventory and saved depleted-node state, then smelting.
Coal is a suggested fuel, not finalized; agree fuel rules and recipes before
implementing them. Authored placements should make sense near rocky areas;
do not scatter deposits randomly or invent progression costs.

## Current limits

Only wood can currently be harvested. Scenery rocks and rune stones are
decorative. Ore, coal, furnace processing, combat, taming and networking are
not implemented. Ground height is a 2D shading foundation; cliffs and explicit
ramp traversal are still future level work. Item dragging, slot selection and
hotbar item use are not yet connected.
