# Creatures v1

Generated with built-in ImageGen; original designs. Runtime extracts 4x4 frames, removes faint alpha padding, resizes to 28x28 native pixels and renders at integer 2x.

Final project asset: assets/art/creatures-v1.png.

Initial prompt:

Production game sprite sheet for original cozy fantasy top-down 2D pixel art RPG. Transparent background. EXACT uniform 4 columns x 4 rows, 16 isolated sprites centered in equal cells, no text, no grid lines. Every row one creature facing RIGHT in four consecutive walk animation frames: idle, left foot forward, idle, right foot forward. Row 1: cute small russet woodland rabbit-deer with short twig antlers and cream belly. Row 2: round sage green meadow slime with leaf cap, squash/stretch animation, friendly expressive eyes. Row 3: luminous small cream-gold night moth with turquoise wing markings, four flapping phases. Row 4: short friendly green goblin villager, large ears, patched brown cloth tunic, simple belt and bare feet, no weapons. Chunky readable pixel clusters, native sprite style 24x24 to 32x32, restrained palette, dark colored outlines, warm highlight upper left, soft dark underside for volume, Stardew-like perspective but entirely original creature designs, no rendered scenery or drop shadows, maintain stable identity and size across each row. Generous transparent padding between cells.

Edit prompt: Keep exactly the same 4x4 sprite sheet, creature designs, poses and grid positions. Remove ALL colored backdrop, haze, glow and bloom BETWEEN and around sprites. Output isolated crisp opaque creature silhouettes on completely transparent empty background, with generous clean alpha-zero padding in every cell. No colored transparent fog. Preserve only the actual creature bodies, leaves, antlers and wings. Pixel art game asset cutouts.

These are ambient prototypes, not approved final Mori/Miru designs. No taming, combat, drops or worker rules introduced. 21 authored inhabitants in 8 habitats; wild positions reset on restart. Goblins wander near camp/fields but do not farm. Ground obstacles are respected, no global pathfinding yet; obstructed walkers choose another local target.
